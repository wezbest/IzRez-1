## 1. Gap Definition

Shariah-compliant origination + MRV (measure-report-verify) stack enabling small halal SMEs (food, cosmetics, light manufacturing, logistics) to issue or join *aggregated fractional green sukuk* (Wakala/Ijara/Murabaha structures) and maintain an audit-ready ESG/impact ledger (energy, water, waste, carbon proxy, halal traceability) verified by banks, buyers, and sukuk investors. Not another sovereign green sukuk; this is the missing-middle layer: pooled SPV sukuk (RM50k–RM500k SME tranches aggregated to RM10M+ issuance) + low-cost ledger mapping inputs once to AAOIFI Shariah governance + ICMA Green Bond Principles + buyer questionnaires [2025](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/25a635f9-254e-4564-9fc7-e895b4918504).

## 2. Root Causes

- **Product mismatch:** Global sukuk reached $264.8B annual issuance in 2025 and $1.37T cumulative, but green/ESG proceeds flow almost exclusively to sovereign/infrastructure megaprojects; standalone SME tickets are uneconomic given legal/SPV/structuring overhead [2025](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/sukuk-market-strong-growth-to-continue-s101664864).
- **No SME MRV:** Small enterprises lack staff and tools to measure, report, and verify emissions; without verified baseline green data, they cannot qualify for sustainability-linked Islamic instruments [2025](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/25a635f9-254e-4564-9fc7-e895b4918504).
- **Dual-compliance burden:** Issuers must satisfy AAOIFI Shariah governance AND ICMA green taxonomies simultaneously; market concentration in four hubs (Saudi, UAE, Malaysia, Indonesia) highlights the difficulty of cross-border compliance [2025](https://www.fitchratings.com/research/non-bank-financial-institutions/esg-sukuk-to-cross-usd50-billion-in-2025-key-esg-funding-role-in-emerging-markets-21-01-2025).
- **Bank risk models:** Islamic financial institutions heavily favour asset-backed, low-risk sovereign and top-tier corporate deals; SMEs are perceived as high-risk due to lack of audited collateral and standardised data [2025](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/25a635f9-254e-4564-9fc7-e895b4918504).
- **Compliance vs impact culture:** IFIs historically concentrated on legalistic negative screens (no riba, gharar, alcohol, pork) rather than proactive carbon/ESG impact metrics [2025](https://journal.inceif.edu.my/index.php/ijif/article/download/210/182).
- **Supply-chain trickle-down:** Large multinationals and tier-1 banks face CSRD/ESRS scope-3 mandates and pass carbon disclosure requirements down to SME suppliers as contractual conditions [2025](https://verdeledger.eu/blog/sustainability-reporting-for-smes).

## 3. Why It Has Not Been Filled

- **Upmarket bookrunner incentives:** Top international sukuk bookrunners (Standard Chartered ~$7.58B, HSBC ~$6.94B/56 issues, DIB ~$6.23B/58 issues in 2025; CIMB domestic corporate ~$9.99B/200+ issues) capture large fees on jumbo sovereign/bank deals, ignoring RM100k SME pools [2025](https://cbonds.com/rankings/1305/4725/?export=pdf&limit=10).
- **Retail tokenisation focuses on buy-side sovereign:** UAE MoF Retail T-Sukuk via ADIB/Emirates Islamic (from ~AED 4,000) and SC Malaysia/Khazanah pilots (RM100 lots) democratise investor access to sovereign paper, not SME sell-side origination [2025](https://www.adib.ae/en/news/2025/apr/abu-dhabi-islamic-bank-becomes-first-bank-to-offer-fractional-sukuk) [2025](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/56808/sc-and-khazanah-to-pilot-bond-and-sukuk-tokenisation-in-2025).
- **Challenge-stage funnels vs standing rails:** Programs like MIFC Global Impact Challenge 2025 (BNM + MLC, 4 winners: e-Wakalah, Gobarakah, Koha, Bagh-e) and IsDB-ILO Youth Green Skills Accelerator remain mentorship funnels, not standing issuance infrastructure [2025](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/mifcgic25).
- **Institutional support is advisory:** BNM Greening Halal Businesses (GHB) hub and Saudi Halal Mark Track offer guidance, but neither provides pooled SPV issuance software [2025](https://www.bnm.gov.my/ghb) [2025](https://www.sustainabilitymea.com/saudi-arabia-introduces-halal-mark-track-to-integrate-esg-standards/).
- **Generic ESG tools ignore Shariah:** Western SME tools (Coolset, Greenly, SME Climate Hub) calculate GHG but lack AAOIFI asset-tracking, halal assurance integration, or zakat/waqf blended-finance features [2025](https://www.efrag.org/en/smes-and-sustainability-reporting).

## 4. Feasibility Analysis

- **Technical:** Highly feasible — web-based MRV intake (bill OCR, utility API, ERP CSV), automated GHG conversion factors (IPCC/DEFRA), and a cryptographically anchored audit trail (SHA-256 hash chains on Postgres), integrated with digital SPV cap-table templates.
- **Shariah:** Feasible — aligns with *Halalan Tayyiban* (wholesome/pure) and *Maqasid al-Shariah* (environmental preservation/maslahah); structured under standard Wakala bi al-Istithmar or Ijara asset-lease where green equipment serves as underlying usufruct.
- **Regulatory:** Feasible via partnership — launch as a technology and analytics provider partnering with an authorized SRI/ESG arranger or securities crowdfunding operator under Malaysia's SC SRI framework or UAE ADGM Green Finance framework, avoiding direct balance-sheet risk.
- **Market:** Outstanding ESG sukuk crossed ~$58B at end-2025 (+30% YoY), projected to exceed $60B by end-2026 with zero historical defaults [2025](https://www.fitchratings.com/research/non-bank-financial-institutions/esg-sukuk-to-cross-usd50-billion-in-2025-key-esg-funding-role-in-emerging-markets-21-01-2025) [2025](https://www.fitchratings.com/research/islamic-finance/esg-sukuk-market-to-surpass-usd60-billion-by-end-2026-no-defaults-29-07-2025).

## 5. Viability Analysis

Unit economics:
1. **MRV SaaS:** RM99–RM399/month ($25–$90/mo) per SME for continuous carbon/ESG passport generation.
2. **Origination/Aggregation fee:** 1.0–2.5% of total pooled sukuk issuance volume.
3. **Servicing fee:** 10–20 bps per annum on outstanding pooled notes for ongoing coupon distribution and MRV impact verification.
4. **Buyer/Bank verification API:** $1,500–$5,000/month enterprise subscription for commercial banks tracking scope-3 supplier emissions.

Break-even achievable on 150 SaaS subscriptions plus one RM10M aggregated issuance per year.

## 6. Survivability Analysis

Moats:
- **Dual Halal-ESG Data Model:** Propitiatory schema linking Halal Assurance System (HAS 23000 / JAKIM / SASO) audits directly to GHG scope 1-2-3 factors.
- **Pre-Structured SPV Shelves:** Pre-cleared master Wakala trust deed templates agreed with Shariah advisory partners, cutting issuance lead time from 6 months to 3 weeks.
- **Bank Channel Lock-In:** Integration with Islamic commercial banks' ESG SME preferential rate desks (e.g., BNM GHB partner banks).

## 8. Pivot Points

- **Pivot A (Pure SaaS Passport):** If pooled capital market syndication faces delays, pivot exclusively to an automated Halal-ESG Supplier Passport for export-oriented SMEs selling to EU/GCC buyers.
- **Pivot B (Financed-Emissions Module):** White-label the MRV engine to regional Islamic commercial banks to calculate their PCAF-aligned portfolio emissions.
- **Pivot C (Blended Zakat-Waqf Facility):** Channel Islamic social finance (cash waqf / corporate zakat) as first-loss credit enhancements for micro-green improvements.

## 9. Acquisition Positioning

Primary acquirers:

- **Global Exchange Operators / Rating Agencies (LSEG, S&P Global, Tadawul Group):** To capture bottom-up private market ESG and sukuk data.

## 10. Zero/Near-Zero Cost MVP Architecture

- **Front-End:** Next.js 15 PWA hosted on Vercel Hobby Tier ($0), Tailwind CSS, multi-language support (English, Bahasa Malaysia, Arabic).
- **Back-End & Database:** Supabase Free Tier (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security, Auth, Storage for utility bill uploads).
- **MRV Computation Engine:** Serverless edge functions computing Scope 1 (fuel litres × factor), Scope 2 (kWh × national grid emission factor), and Scope 3 proxy (spend-based).
- **OCR Ingestion:** Client-side Tesseract.js for scanning electricity/water bills and halal certificates directly in-browser.
- **Verification Integrity:** SHA-256 hash chaining on all MRV data records with daily root hash published to Polygon Amoy testnet ($0).
- **Structuring Tool:** Automated SPV cap-table generator creating standardized PDF term sheets and AAOIFI-compliant Wakala agreements.

## 15. Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregation SPV regulatory delay | Moderate | High | Partner with licensed securities crowdfunding / digital asset exchanges rather than incorporating de-novo. |
| Greenwashing scrutiny on proxies | Moderate | High | Restrict metrics to verified Scope 1 & 2 utility data; use audited DEFRA/IPCC factors; partner with accredited SPO providers. |
| SME onboarding friction | High | Moderate | Automate bill extraction via OCR and WhatsApp photo uploads to keep setup under 10 minutes. |
| Shariah board divergence | Low | High | Use classical Wakala bi al-Istithmar structure pre-cleared by established scholars from AAOIFI / SAC. |

## 16. Startup Name Rationale

**TayyibLedger** — Combines *Tayyib* (Arabic for wholesome, pure, ethically sound — the theological foundation for Islamic sustainability) with *Ledger* (denoting transparent, verifiable accounting). Clear, institutional, and values-aligned across Arabic, English, and Southeast Asian markets.

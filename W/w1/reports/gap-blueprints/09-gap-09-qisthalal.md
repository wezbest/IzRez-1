## 1. Gap Definition

Short-ticket halal BNPL (2–4 instalments / ≤12-mo Deferred Payment Credit, commodity-Murabaha, fixed profit, no compounding, SRB-certified) + ijara vehicle/asset financing (lease-to-own, *Ijara wa Iqtina*, provider owns + bears risk, fixed rentals, title via gift/sale). Diaspora wedge: UK Muslims (~82% seek Sharia products) forced "cash or nothing"; GCC youth prefer instalments. Single app: certified BNPL + larger ijara + scholar transparency ledger.

## 2. Root Causes

- **Creed-structure mismatch:** PCP/HP/loans and conventional BNPL = loan-plus-APR; Sharia needs trade/asset-ownership profit, fixed upfront [2025](https://www.ayan.co.uk/blog-post/guide-to-halal-car-finance-in-the-uk) [2025](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/transactions-shafii-fiqh/is-pcp-car-finance-considered-interest-or-a-permissible-lease-in-islam/) [2025](https://tabby.sa/en-SA/toc/murabaha).
- **Capital intensity:** ijara needs balance sheet + residual/servicing risk; only de-risked at scale — Ayan £75M senior facility [2026](https://www.ayan.co.uk/blog-post/ayan-triple-point-facility) [2026](https://dealroom.co/news/149687-triple-point-backs-ayan-capital-with-75m-shariah-compliant-facility/).
- **Graduation cliff 2025–2026:** SAMA consumer/SME licences [2025](https://tamara.co/en-sa/blog-post/sama-finance-license) [2026](https://tabby.ai/en-AE/newsroom/finance-licences-ksa); CBUAE Short-Term Credit + SVF [2025](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/uae-central-bank-introduces-regulatory-framework-buy-now-pay-later-providers); CBB BNPL Module consultation Nov 2025 [2025](https://www.cbb.gov.bh/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Consultation_Proposed-Buy-Now-Pay-Later-Module.pdf); UK FCA DPC PS26/1, Regulation Day 15 Jul 2026 [2026](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps26-1-regulation-deferred-payment-credit).
- **Trust deficit:** "re-badged interest" fear needs SRB/Dar Al Marajaa cert + published commodity flow + purified late fees (Tabby SRB [2025](https://tabby.sa/en-SA/help-business/about-tabby/shariah-compliant); Flooss Dar Al Marajaa [2026](https://waya.media/bahrains-flooss-secures-usd-22m-credit-facility-to-scale-consumer-finance/)).
- **Duopoly:** Tabby (25m users/70k merchants/>$18B volume, $233M Series F at $6.5B [2026](https://tabby.ai/en-AE/newsroom/series-f)); Tamara ($2.4B facility Sep 2025 [2025](https://tamara.co/en-sa/blog-post/tamara-secures-up-to-2-4-billion-dollar-facility)); UK £20B+ used-car finance left to PCP [2025](https://www.kandoo.co.uk/guides/no-interest-car-finance-for-muslims).

## 3. Why It Has Not Been Filled

Ticket-size chasm: Tabby/Tamara optimised checkout (4-pay → SAR 50k Murabaha monthly post-Jun-2026 licences); Ayan proved ijara but UK-only prime cars, no BNPL flywheel. Funding only arrived 2025–2026 (Tabby $700M JPM 2023 [2023](https://tabby.ai/en-AE/newsroom/jpmorgan-series-d-extension); Tamara $2.4B; Flooss $22M Shorooq Jan 2026 [2026](https://en.incarabia.com/bahrainbased-shariahcompliant-fintech-flooss-raises-us22-million-credit-facility-from-shorooq-815552.html)). X-border licence fragmentation + thin-file underwriting + merchant economics (need 70k-merchant scale) block newcomers.

## 4. Feasibility Analysis

- **Technical:** feasible broker-first — checkout widget + ijara marketplace + affordability stub (TrueLayer/BenefitPay sandbox) + versioned Murabaha/Ijara templates + charity-purified late ledger.
- **Shariah:** feasible — commodity-Murabaha flow + ijara ownership risk + external audit; publish receipts + purification ledger.
- **Regulatory:** SA 6 / UAE 6 / UK 7 — paths proven (Tamara SAMA Mar 2025; Tabby Jun 2026; Flooss CBB; Ayan FCA) but now full-licence; MVP must operate as introducer/broker under partner permissions + UK merchant-own DPC exemption.
- **Market:** MENA BNPL $5.79B→$20B+ 2025, 19–30% YoY [2025](https://synapse-analytics.io/blog/bnpl-in-mena-gcc-growth-drivers-market-dynamics-and-the-road-ahead); UK halal-auto gap acute.

## 5. Viability Analysis

Merchant discount/introducer commission + ijara brokerage + AyanCare-style attach + plugin SaaS + scoring API; later net margin + sukuk spread. Proven: Tabby profitable since 2023; Tamara profitable 9M 2025. Minus 2 for warehouse capital + s.75/forbearance cost.

## 6. Survivability Analysis

Moat: published scholar rulings + commodity receipts + purification ledger (Klarna can't copy credibly) + PHV/fleet niches + GCC↔UK bridge. Duopoly squeeze risk — defend via ijara depth + broker network.

## 8. Pivot Points

Pure Sharia-audit SaaS for BNPL lenders; takaful-backed PHV/fleet ijara (B2B); diaspora remittance-to-asset; merchant-own DPC enablement (exempt).

## 9. Acquisition Positioning

Tabby/Tamara (ijara + UK bridge), Ayan (BNPL funnel), Flooss/Shorooq roll-up, Al Rayan/GCC banks (auto channel), Triple Point-type private credit. Timing: 50 funded ijara + 500 BNPL checkouts <5% 30dpd.

## 10. Zero/Near-Zero Cost MVP Architecture

Broker-first, no balance sheet. Next.js/Vercel free + Supabase (users, merchants, applications, commodity tickets, ijara schedules, RLS) + Stripe Links (UK test) / partner API (GCC) + Experian sandbox + TrueLayer/BenefitPay sandbox + Resend/WhatsApp nudges + Metabase delinquency dashboards. Publish CONC-style disclosure + s.75 explainer (UK post-Jul-2026). Flows: UK used-car dealer → partner ijara quotes; GCC Shopify plugin → partner Murabaha BNPL.

## 11. MVP Presentation Strategy

Demo: halal checkout (Murabaha disclosure + scholar note) + ijara quote (ownership schedule) + affordability check. Narrative: "Why PCP is haram, how ijara differs — with receipts." Proof: approval %, take-rate, halal-confidence NPS.

## 12. Contact Targets

SAMA-licensed arrangers; CBB Volume 5 desks; DIFC/ADGM partner banks; UK halal dealers + PHV fleets. Individual emails: NOT RETRIEVED.

## 13. Monetization Methods
